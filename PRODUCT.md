## Platform
web

## Stack
Next.js App Router, React, strict TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and the supporting libraries named in the supplied brief.

## Users
Undergraduate Operating Systems students are the primary users. Professors, educators, and independent learners use the product as a visual teaching aid.

## Product Purpose
SeekScope is a browser-based learning platform for exploring traditional HDD cylinder scheduling. Learners configure a request queue, run an algorithm, follow its movement trace, verify total head movement, compare six algorithms, and study their behavior.

## Positioning
An interactive laboratory that makes every seek and its contribution to movement visible in a single trace-driven experience.

## Operating Context
Public, no sign-in required; simulations run locally in the browser. Inputs and preloaded queues are illustrative examples.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The MVP covers FCFS, SSTF, SCAN, C-SCAN, LOOK, and C-LOOK on a simplified traditional mechanical HDD cylinder model. It does not inspect or control physical disks, model SSD/NVMe, measure real latency or throughput, or connect to a server, device, or API. The full authoritative conventions and examples are in `C:/Users/yashv/Downloads/seekscope-product-brief.md`.

## Evidence on Hand
Only the user-supplied product brief and its specified illustrative regression workload. No endorsements, user metrics, customer list, or external performance evidence is supplied.

## Product Principles
- Make algorithm decisions visible as cylinder movement.
- Derive visualization, trace, and totals from the same execution data.
- Explain rules and edge cases with concrete examples and learner prediction.
- Label illustrative data and model limits honestly.
- Keep the experience public and client-side.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Responsive layouts, keyboard-operable controls, readable contrast, and reduced-motion support are required by the brief.
